# My Hands

Do you feel heavy

I want to hold you

with my hands

cut away your unease with a winter wind like a blade

and let your good dreams stay the night

Are you tired

I want you to cry all you need, and sleep in peace

between my hands

When the night has soaked the hem of an old dream

when a bowl of congee steams the morning light soft

I hope my hands

are not only the glow of lamps in the south

but can also be a fire close at hand

lighting your hands

and letting them light me

2012.12
