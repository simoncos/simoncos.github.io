Research

Questions I was curious about, worked through with data and sources.

League of Legends: The map of champion matchupsAn interactive essay on every champion pair from 222,616 ranked League of Legends matches: 606 of 8,128 pairs show a clear edge, nearly all pointing from the stronger to the weaker champion. Champion strength explains about 97% of pair win-rate differences and pair-specific interaction about 1.2%; no rock-paper-scissors cycles. Predictive, not causal; exploratory.2026 League of Legends: Does the draft decide the game?An interactive essay from 222,652 ranked League of Legends matches: the draft alone predicts the winner only modestly (AUC about 0.57), the first 10–20 minutes far better.
