Flower of the Void虛無之花

虛無之花Flower of the Void

趙澈 Che Zhao · simoncos

From an old dream从一场旧梦出发

A finite life in a seemingly boundless universe. We wake from chaos, but the dream’s emotions linger, blooming into a black flower.有限的个体，看似无垠的宇宙。从混沌中醒来，梦里的情绪仍留在身体里，绽放成一朵黑花。

It began as a piece I wrote in 2012, drawn from my own dreams. I spent dozens of hours working with AI, reviewing and refining the film to give those dreams sound and form. This version leaves the images and sound room to speak.它来自真实梦境，也来自我2012年写下的同名文章。我与AI协作，花了数十小时反复观看、反馈和调整，让这场旧梦有了声音与画面。这个版本把更多解释空间留给声音与画面。

Making the film制作记

Dreams, happy accidents, and the decisions that shaped the film.真实梦境、意外收获，以及一次次塑造影片的选择。
