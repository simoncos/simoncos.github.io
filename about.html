I am a Hong Kong-based builder working across software, AI, data, research, and lived field notes. This site collects the tools, essays, experiments, talks, and field notes that grow out of that work.

Motto Connecting the dots.

What I do

I work through four modes.

Build Make usable tools

I turn recurring problems into small systems, dashboards, prototypes, and public utilities.

Research Structure messy questions

I use evidence, timelines, comparisons, and constraints to make complex topics easier to inspect.

Systems Design personal infrastructure

I care about workflows, memory, agents, publishing, feedback loops, and the boundaries between them.

Field Keep contact with reality

Outdoor experience, body data, health, places, and risk keep the work grounded outside screens.

Operating principles

The through-line is not a topic. It is a way of working.

Make the work inspectable: expose sources, assumptions, states, and next questions instead of only final outputs.

Use AI as a collaborator with boundaries: useful for pressure, synthesis, review, and automation, but not a substitute for judgment.

Keep the site useful as the work changes: recent work should move forward, and older work should remain easy to find.
